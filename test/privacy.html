Privacy

Last updated October 2026

Short version: I only keep what you give me, I only use it for what you asked, and you can have it deleted any time.

Who looks after your data

Patrick, who writes Madrid Unpacked. Questions or requests: patrickjharris91@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Your email, if you sign up for a guide. Nothing else is asked for.
  • Your messages, if you WhatsApp or email me. They stay in WhatsApp or my inbox.
  • Booking details for the €150 call: your name and the date we agree.

What I do with it

Send the one email you signed up for, reply to you, and run the call you booked. That's it.

Reading preferences on your device

The website remembers your last guide and chapter, the sections you save and your visit-checklist ticks on this device. Remove saved sections in the Saved panel, use Reset checklist, or clear this site’s data in your browser to remove them. Your question draft, selected section and town-picker answers stay in this tab’s session storage; the website does not send your draft to Patrick. You send it yourself in WhatsApp or your email app.

What I never do

  • Sell, rent or share your email.
  • Add you to anything you didn't ask for.
  • Show ads, or use advertising trackers.

How long I keep it

Your email stays until you unsubscribe (every email has the link), then it's deleted. Messages are deleted on request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you — just email me. If you're not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).
