About me

Hi there! My name is Patrick, a lawyer-turned-tech-worker from the UK. I've lived in Spain for over a decade. Just over a year ago we made a big move: with our two small children in tow, we moved from a town outside Barcelona to a town outside Madrid.

So enough about me — welcome to Madrid Unpacked! The concept is as simple as it gets: take everything I actually learned moving from Barcelona to Madrid Norte, and organise it into guides so people going through the same move can do so with every last question answered by someone who lived it.

Moving with small children is straight-up terrifying. Exciting, but terrifying. It's a process full of second-guessing, self-doubt, and taking risks. When we were deep in the process of moving, I was shocked by just how hard it was to find real answers to crucial questions about the different towns. That's what inspired Madrid Unpacked. I'll add guides progressively, but for now I want to keep it hyper-focused on my personal experience — which means limiting it to Madrid Norte.

My aim is to answer the questions when Chat GPT and Claude just don't cut it, based on my own lived, practical experience. I researched the towns, searched the schools myself, hunted the housing, bought a place off-plan, compared mortgages, and lived through every piece of paperwork Spain could throw at us. I don't have the t-shirt, but I do have my first wrinkles and grey hairs to show for it — hopefully something Madrid Unpacked can prevent for you.

Jokes aside, none of it was catastrophic, but a lot of it would have been easier with someone honest telling us what to actually expect.

These days, life mostly looks like: half working, half ferrying kids between whichever park has become the current favourite, and trying but failing to move beyond mediocre at tennis. I'm not a relocation company, and I'm certainly not a lawyer offering legal advice here — just someone who's done this move, taking notes the whole way through so you don't have to start from zero.

If you're somewhere in the middle of your own version of this — figuring out schools, neighbourhoods, or just whether any of this is a good idea — that's exactly who these guides are for.
